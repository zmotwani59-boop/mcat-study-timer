Student research toolkit

Make the most of the fair.

Whether you're exploring for the first time or actively searching for a lab, start with curiosity.

Questions to ask

  • What is your group trying to figure out?
  • What would an undergraduate do on your team?
  • Do I need prior lab or coding experience?
  • How much time do you expect each week?
  • How should I follow up after today?

After the fair

  1. Write down the name of the person you met.
  2. Note what genuinely interested you.
  3. Use the group's website-listed contact or application.
  4. Send a brief, specific follow-up within a few days.
  5. Keep exploring, even if a group isn't recruiting.
