What we do

GUMAPS gives students practical ways to find community, build experience, meet mentors, and serve beyond the Hilltop.

MENTORSHIP

Peer mentorship

Undergraduate mentor matching, informal check-ins, coffee chats, and support navigating Georgetown and pre-health life.

SERVICE

Community service

Group opportunities with organizations across DC, with public signups and new dates added throughout the semester.

OPPORTUNITY

Research, skills & shadowing

Research connections, clinical skills programming, physician outreach, and guidance for building meaningful experience.
