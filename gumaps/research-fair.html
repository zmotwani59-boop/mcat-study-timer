Starting a conversation

You don't have to know everything about a lab before stopping by its table.

A few questions to ask

  1. What question is your lab working on?
  2. What does an undergraduate work on in the group?
  3. What skills would be helpful to learn?
  4. What's the best way to follow up?

After the fair

Write down the name of the person you met and one thing you found interesting. If the group has an application, use the link above. Otherwise, send a short email referencing your conversation.

Not every lab is taking students right now. You can still ask about their projects and whether there may be opportunities later.
